# ISSN review queue: notes on the 2026-10-23 decisions

The nine rows of `open-issues.csv` were decided by the owner on 2026-10-23 and applied by
`scripts/apply-issn-queue-decisions.mjs` (website issue #102). The owner checked every ISSN again the same day against
the ISSN Portal, the publishers, DOAJ and NLM; all 17 ISSNs taken over pass their check digit. Points to keep in mind:

- **POSI-J-013785 Revista de Biologia Neotropical.** The ISSN Portal record gives 1807-9652 (print, ISSN-L) and
  2178-0579 (online). 1806-9652 was a typo of the print ISSN.
- **POSI-J-000143 / POSI-J-000173.** Two different journals. The live record of POSI-J-000143 (journals/core) already
  had Frontiers in Environmental Science's ISSN 2296-665X; its stale copy in journals/discovered carried
  2813-5067 and the website of Frontiers in Environmental Engineering and is now corrected. POSI-J-000173 keeps
  2813-5067 (ISSN-L, online), OpenAlex S4387285408 and its own website. After the change no ISSN is held by two live ids
  (validator: 0 under review).
- **Hydrology Research (POSI-J-025089).** Owned by IWA Publishing and the Nordic Association for Hydrology. From 2026
  Elsevier provides publishing services, and articles published after 1 March 2026 are on ScienceDirect. The record keeps
  iwaponline.com/hr as the journal's home page; a ScienceDirect link is not wrong.
- **Chinese Journal of Population, Resources and Environment (POSI-J-024548).** Current ISSNs per KeAi and the ISSN
  Portal: print 2096-9589, online 2325-4262 (ISSN-L). 1004-2857 is not a typo: the ISSN Portal registers it separately
  (key title "China Population, Resources & Environment (Ji’nan)"), while Taylor & Francis and China's national
  sci-tech journal platform still list it as this journal's print ISSN. It is not given to POSI-J-024548; it stays on
  the superseded POSI-J-003672 as history. Revisit if the two titles are shown to be one serial.
- **Scientia Horticulturae (POSI-J-023668).** The Portal records the print edition up to 2024; 0304-4238 remains the
  print ISSN and the ISSN-L.

Superseded ids (`registry/superseded-ids.csv`) are not deleted: their records, provenance and history stay in
`journals/discovered`; the registry maps each old id to its survivor, and the old id is left out of the live records.
